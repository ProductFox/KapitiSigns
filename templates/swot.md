# Product SWOT

Where our product stands in its market. Opportunities and threats come from the radar, with sources. Strengths and weaknesses come from product.md or from the product owner. Anything the agent could not source is marked "Needs the product owner's input" and is not treated as fact.

## Summary

Three or four sentences: our strongest position, our biggest exposure, and the single most important thing to act on.

## Opportunities
