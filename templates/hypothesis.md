# HYP-000: Short name for the problem

## Problem

What is going wrong for people, in their words. Describe the problem, not a solution. If the input proposed a solution, work back to the problem underneath it.

## Who has it

Which people or organisations have this problem. Be as specific as the evidence allows.

## How often

How often they run into it.

## What it costs them

Time, money, risk, stress or missed outcomes.

## What we believe

We believe that [who] struggle with [problem] because [reason], and that solving it would [outcome].

## Evidence that would prove us wrong

What we would need to see to drop this hypothesis.

## Original input

The raw input as received, quoted or summarised, with where it came from and the date.

## Open questions

Anything marked "Unknown" above, written as a question to answer later.
